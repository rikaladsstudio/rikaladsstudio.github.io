# Memory #1: How I Got Into LADS

![My epic reply to my friend's message](/chat-excerpt.png)
*My epic reply to my friend's message*

*This is part of Rika's Memories series. Some details may be fuzzy as this is written from my memory. Chat dialogue is recreated and paraphrased, and some details are changed to protect privacy.*

I've realized I'm getting more forgetful as I get older, so I thought I'd start jotting down my memories as a Love and Deepspace (LADS) player. Think of this as a trip down memory lane. I'm doing my best to recall everything accurately, though it's been nearly three years since January 2024!

This series may be a little boring, since there won't be any drama, ragebait, or hot takes. It's just me reminiscing and sharing the things I've come to appreciate.

---

It was January 17, 2024, the day before the game's official release. A friend sent me a message:
> There's a new mobile otome game called Love and Deepspace and there's CHARACTER CUSTOMIZATION
![Recreated chat: Friend excitedly says a new otome game, Love and Deepspace, has character customization](/chat-excerpt.png)
*My epic reply to my friend's message*

She even attached a screenshot of her MC to show me!

I'd just finished a k-drama she had recommended, which I really enjoyed. That explains the first image, where we're laughing about one of its absurd scenes. Ah, classic makjang~😆

![Scene from Perfect Marriage](/kdrama-scene.png)
*That k-drama scene from Perfect Marriage*

I wasn't sure what to do next to pass the time. I could've watched another TV show, but somehow I ended up downloading LADS instead.

When it comes to recommendations from friends, I’m usually willing to give something a try, as long as I’m comfortable with it. And this was one of those times!

I'd played Chinese gacha and otome mobile games on global servers before, so it wasn't exactly my first rodeo. I had only played a few otome games, mainly because I'm not fond of reading text, but I was already familiar with the genre from reviews and spoilers. I was also gradually learning more about Chinese media, even practicing my Chinese and picking up some internet slang.

And that's how my LADS journey began!
